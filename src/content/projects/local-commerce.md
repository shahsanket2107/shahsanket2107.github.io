During the lockdowns, small merchants in tier three Indian cities lost the only channel they
had, which was people walking into the shop. Two of us built them somewhere to sell online. It
ended up serving around six hundred customers.

Most of the engineering was inventory. Merchants were selling things they had already sold,
because nothing was keeping stock in step across the shop and the app.

We never turned it into a business. It served real people for a while and then it ended. We
built what we assumed merchants needed and then spent months correcting that in production.
